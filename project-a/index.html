JellyyFish

Scientific Name:

Oculuna Marina

Discovered by / at:

Samira, CCLab Fall 2026

Family:

Oculunidae

Habitat:

Shallow, calm coastal waters, where nothing could potentially disturb it. Unless..

Appearance:

The creature's upper half is a wide, translucent dome, while its lower half consists of a cluster of long, flowing tentacles that trail behind it as it moves. Its body is covered in iridescent scales that shimmer in the water. Its stalked eyes are capable of glowing to communicate with other members of its species in case of any danger.

Key Characteristics:

  • Swims calmly through the water.
  • Retracts its eyes when threatened.
  • Emits bioluminescent light through its eyes for communication.
  • Reacts curiosuly to nearby movement. But don't get too close.
  • Attempts to blend into its surroundings to escape any danger.
  • Body changes color depending on the water temperature.

What we know so far about this specie of JellyyFish Oculuna Marina is a rare and elusive species of jellyfish that has only recently been discovered in the shallow coastal waters of the Pacific Ocean. Its unique ability to make its eyes glow allows it to communicate with other members of its species, while its gelatinous body allows it to blend seamlessly into its surroundings, making it difficult for predators to spot. Scientists are still studying this fascinating species to learn more about its behavior, diet, and reproductive habits. When threatened, Oculuna retracts its eye stalks into its body as a defense mechanism. Oculuna appears cautious around unfamiliar movements but her curious nature gets the better of her. Its keen eyesight allows it to detect potential threats in its environment.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Be respectful of its habitat and avoid touching or disturbing it. If you encounter an Oculuna Marina, observe it from a safe distance and avoid making sudden movements that could startle it. Do not attempt to feed or capture the creature, as this can cause stress and harm to its delicate body.
